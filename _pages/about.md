I am a postdoctoral researcher in the probabilistic machine learning group at Saarland University. My PhD work studied collaboration as a principle for improving machine learning: on the modeling side, I developed a non-adversarial framework that cooperatively trains a proxy model to symmetrize the KL divergence, and on the societal side, I studied algorithmic collective action: how individuals can coordinate data modifications to influence trained models, including practical methods for minorities to reduce unfairness. I am interested in the two-way relationship between ML and the world: what we can learn from data, and how people and ML systems shape each other.
